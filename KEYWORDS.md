# Keyword map

One page, one primary phrase. Written down so nobody has to guess what a page is for,
and so two pages never compete for the same search.

Researched 2026-10-01 by reading the live results for these searches. Volume and
difficulty figures are **not** in here: that needs Ahrefs, Semrush, or the query data
in Search Console, none of which was available when this was written. Treat the
mapping as informed, not measured, until it is checked against Search Console.

## What the results actually look like

Searching "best digital marketing agency in Bangladesh", "social media marketing agency
in Dhaka" and "branding agency in Dhaka" returns the same kind of page almost all the
way down: **directories, not agencies.** Clutch, DesignRush, Sortlist, Semrush Agency
Partners, G2 and Dribbble hold those results. Agency homepages barely appear.

Searching "video editing agency Bangladesh" is different. There, agency pages do rank —
Libanza Films holds several spots with pages built exactly for the phrase, at URLs like
`/video-editing-company-in-bangladesh`.

Two conclusions, and they pull in different directions:

1. **For "best [service] agency in Bangladesh", the way in is to be listed** on Clutch,
   DesignRush, Sortlist, GoodFirms and Semrush Agency Partners. Those pages rank; being
   on them puts us in the result. Outranking them with our own homepage is a long fight
   for a search where the reader wants a list anyway.
2. **For specific service phrases, our own pages can win.** That is what the seven
   service pages are for, and it is where the on-page work below belongs.

## The map

| Page | Primary phrase | Also covers |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `/` | marketing agency in Bangladesh | creative agency, branding agency, Gen Z marketing |
| `/services/brand-identity/` | branding agency in Bangladesh | logo design, brand identity, brand book |
| `/services/video-production/` | video editing agency in Bangladesh | video production, reels, motion graphics, product photography |
| `/services/web-design/` | web design agency Bangladesh | website development, responsive build |
| `/services/influencer-marketing/` | influencer marketing agency in Bangladesh | creator partnerships, campaign reporting |
| `/services/growth-marketing/` | digital marketing agency in Bangladesh | Facebook ads, Google Ads, Meta ads, performance marketing, attribution |
| `/services/social-media-marketing/` | social media marketing agency in Bangladesh | social media management, content calendar |
| `/services/recruitment-support/` | employer branding Bangladesh | recruitment marketing, job adverts |

Each primary phrase appears in that page's title, H1, meta description, opening
paragraph and at least one answer. Density sits between 0.2% and 0.6% of the visible
words, which is ordinary prose rather than stuffing. Stuffing is worth avoiding twice
over: Google ignores it, and the Princeton GEO study found it *reduces* how often AI
engines cite a page.

## Known gaps

- **Bangla and Banglish searches.** The site is entirely in English. A large share of
  Bangladeshi buyers search in Bangla or in romanised Bangla. Nothing here addresses
  that, and nobody has measured how much traffic it costs.
- **Dhaka as its own search.** "marketing agency in Dhaka" is a different query from
  "in Bangladesh". The service pages mention Dhaka where it is true, but there is no
  page built for the city. A Google Business Profile is the better answer for this
  before a page is.
- **No informational content.** Every page here is a service page, which catches people
  ready to hire. Nothing catches the person searching "how much does branding cost in
  Bangladesh" six weeks earlier.

## Closed since first written

- **Ad platforms are named.** We run Meta ads (Facebook and Instagram) and Google Ads,
  confirmed 2026-10-01. The growth page, the service line itself, the homepage answer,
  llms.txt and the organisation schema all say so, which puts us in reach of "Facebook
  ads agency Bangladesh" and "Google Ads agency Bangladesh".

## Before changing this

Check it against Search Console (Performance → Queries) once the new pages have a month
of data. Real queries beat a sensible guess, and some of the guesses above will be
wrong.
